# Customer Churn & Revenue Risk Analysis

## Business Objective
Identify customer churn drivers and quantify revenue at risk to support data-driven retention strategies.

## Dataset
Telecommunications customer data including tenure, contract type, payment method, charges, and churn status.

## Tools Used
- SQL Server – data cleaning, churn metrics, segmentation
- Power BI – KPI reporting and executive dashboard
- Excel – intermediate KPI outputs

## Key KPIs
- Churn Rate: ~26.5%
- Retention Rate: ~73.5%
- ARPU: ~$64.8
- Monthly Revenue at Risk: ~$139K

## Key Insights
- Month-to-month and early-tenure customers exhibit the highest churn.
- Electronic check payment method has the highest churn rate.
- High-charge customers contribute disproportionately to revenue at risk.

## Business Recommendations
- Target early-tenure customers with retention incentives.
- Promote long-term contracts to reduce churn.
- Prioritize high-value customers for proactive retention campaigns.

## Deliverables
- SQL scripts for churn analysis (`/sql`)
- Power BI dashboard and screenshots (`/powerbi`)
- Excel KPI outputs (`/excel`)
